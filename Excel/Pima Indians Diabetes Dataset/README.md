## Healthcare Diabetes Risk Dashboard Using Power BI

## Business Problem

**  Healthcare providers want to identify the key factors associated with 
diabetes and monitor high-risk patients for early intervention.

## Dashboard Objective

## Create an interactive dashboard that answers:

** What percentage of patients have diabetes?
-- Do diabetic patients have higher glucose levels?
-- Does BMI influence diabetes occurrence?
-- Are older patients more likely to have diabetes?
-- Which patient groups are most at risk?

## Key Findings
-- Glucose is the strongest indicator of diabetes.
-- Diabetic patients exhibit higher BMI levels.
-- Diabetes prevalence increases with age.
-- Family history contributes to diabetes risk.
-- Patients with elevated glucose and BMI are highest risk.

## Recommendations
-- Increase diabetes screening among patients aged 40+.
-- Promote weight management initiatives.
-- Monitor patients with elevated glucose levels.
-- Include family history in risk assessments.
-- Develop preventive care programs for high-risk individuals.


## Exploratory Data Analysis (Excel)

** Before building the Power BI dashboard, 
exploratory analysis was performed using "Excel Pivot Tables".

## Key findings included:

Diabetes prevalence of 35%.
Diabetic patients had significantly higher average glucose levels.
Diabetic patients had higher average BMI values.
Diabetic patients were older on average.
Patients with stronger family history indicators showed higher diabetes occurrence.
Diabetes prevalence increased in middle-aged and senior groups.
