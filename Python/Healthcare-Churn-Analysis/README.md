## Healthcare Patient Churn Analysis & Prediction | Power BI + Python

##  Project Overview

Patient retention is a major challenge in the healthcare sector.
High churn rates can lead to poor patient outcomes and revenue loss.

This project analyzes patient behavior and predicts churn using data analyticsand machine learning. 
The goal is to identify high-risk patients and provide actionable insights to improve retention.

---

##  Objectives

* Analyze key factors influencing patient churn
* Identify high-risk patients likely to leave
* Build a predictive model for churn classification
* Provide data-driven recommendations for patient retention

---

##  Dashboard Highlights

* **Total Patients:** 2,000
* **Churned Patients:** 1,367
* **Churn Rate:** 68.35%
* **High-Risk Patients Identified:** 337
* **Average Churn Probability:** 0.52

---

##  Key Insights

* Patients with **low satisfaction (≤ 3)** are highly likely to churn
* **Billing issues** significantly increase churn risk
* Patients with **low portal usage** show higher churn behavior
* A total of **337 high-risk patients** were identified for targeted intervention

---

##  Recommendations

* Improve patient satisfaction through better healthcare experience
* Address billing-related issues promptly
* Encourage use of digital health portals for engagement
* Focus retention strategies on high-risk patient groups

---

##  Machine Learning Model

* **Model Used:** Logistic Regression
* **Purpose:** Predict likelihood of patient churn (Churn = 1, Stay = 0)
* **Output:** Probability score indicating churn risk
