## Project Title
Healthcare Diabetes Risk Analysis Using SQL

## Business Problem
A healthcare organization wants to identify 
the characteristics most associated with diabetes
so that high-risk patients can be identified
early and preventive interventions can be implemented.

##  Business Question
1. What percentage of patients have diabetes?
2. Do diabetic patients have higher glucose levels?
3. Does BMI influence diabetes occurrence?
4. Are older patients more likely to have diabetes?
5. Which patient groups are most at risk?


## Key Findings
1. Diabetic patients had significantly higher average glucose levels.
2. Higher BMI was associated with diabetes.
3. Older patients exhibited greater diabetes prevalence.
4. Family history contributed to increased diabetes risk.
5. Patients with both elevated BMI and glucose levels represented the highest-risk group.

## Recommendations
1. Increase diabetes screening among patients over 40 years old.
2. Implement weight-management programs for high-BMI patients.
3. Monitor patients with elevated glucose levels more frequently.
4. Incorporate family-history assessments into routine care.
5. Develop risk-based intervention programs for high-risk patients.
